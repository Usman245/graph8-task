# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Sales reps, agency account managers, and delivery leads reviewing negotiated work in Graph8.

## Product Purpose

Keep seller commitments consistent with quotes and make accepted commitments actionable for delivery.
Success means a reviewer can inspect the evidence, address a discrepancy, and track the resulting obligation.

## Brand Personality

Direct, careful, practical. Follow the existing application's plain-language voice.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative dashboards, unsupported savings estimates, and green states that conceal incomplete evidence.

## Design Principles

- Show evidence beside consequential decisions.
- Preserve conditions and distinguish a buyer request from a seller commitment.
- Make the next action explicit and keep state in Graph8.
- Preserve the existing components, colors, and navigation conventions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic controls, visible focus, explicit labels, text alongside status colors, responsive layouts,
and announced loading/error states. These are implementation requirements, not a claim of a completed accessibility audit.
